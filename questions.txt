1. What is ambient lighting?
      Ambient lighting is the general lighting that hits all shapes.
2. What is diffuse lighting?
      Diffues lighting is directional lighting. It hits certain faces of the shapes and is affected by distance.
3. Why do we need normal vectors?
      We need normal vectors to be able to see the lighting. If the vectors aren't normal the inside of the object gets light, not the visible outside.
4. What role does the dot product play in lighting calculations?
      It is used in the calculation of light color.
5. How does changing light direction affect a 3D object?
      Changing the light direction changes which planes of the shape get diffuse lighting.
6. How does changing light color affect realism?
      Changing the light color affects realism by allowing showing highlights to simulate things like spotlights.
7. Which modification produced the most interesting result?
      The modification that produced the most intersting results in my opinion was the color changeing.
8. Why do game engines automate lighting calculations?
      They automate lighting calculations to make lighting more user-friendly so those who don't know the calculations can still implement lighting.
